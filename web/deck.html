Why this is only possible now

Two years ago the cheap tier could not do the job at all.

2024

Small open models failed mechanical work outright. Routing had no upside, so nobody built the governor.

2026

Open weights cleared the bar. Nebius Token Factory puts 24 of them behind one endpoint at a published per-token price you can compute against.

The gap is now hundreds of times — and the quality difference is noise.

prices read live from the Token Factory API · GET /v1/models?verbose=true

What you actually do with it

One command. Then one line to change.

Step 1 — see it

npx ax-route audit

No key. No account. Reads the logs your tools already keep.

Step 2 — change it

~/.claude/agents/memory-retrieval.md
- model: opus
+ model: haiku

Or point Cursor at Route and it switches by itself.

It tells you which files to change, and why.

And it refuses to tell you about the other five jobs, because it has not tested them.

Every routed call leaves a receipt: model, tokens, cost, region.

The saving is an invoice, not a claim.

The business

Cost is the wedge. Residency is the moat.

Nebius serves from Finland. For a European company this is one product solving two problems: the budget line, and the contract that says source code cannot leave the EU.

Open-source router. Commercial control plane: shared policies, spend limits, team ledgers, escalation. It sits in the request path, so the value scales with how much you use agents.
