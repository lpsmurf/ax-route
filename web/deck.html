Why this is only possible now

Two years ago the cheap tier could not do the job at all.

2024

Small open models failed mechanical work outright. Routing had no upside, so nobody built the governor.

2026

Open weights cleared the bar. Nebius Token Factory puts 24 of them behind one endpoint at a published per-token price you can compute against.

The gap is now hundreds of times — and the quality difference is noise.

What you actually do with it

One command. Then one line to change.

Step 1 — see it

npx ax-route audit

No key. No account. Reads the logs your tools already keep.

Step 2 — change it

~/.claude/agents/memory-retrieval.md
- model: opus
+ model: haiku

Or point Cursor at Route and it switches by itself.

It tells you which files to change, and why.

And it refuses to tell you about the other five jobs, because it has not tested them.

Every routed call leaves a receipt: model, tokens, cost, region.

Every figure here traces back to a logged call you can open.

Business model

Teams

Control plane

Shared policies, spend caps, per-project ledgers, approvals. Seat-based, for the people who have to sign off on the bill.

How it spreads

Every run ends with a figure people want to post. npx ax-route audit costs nothing to try and needs no sign-up, so it travels through the Discords and timelines these users already live in. They land as individuals; their teams follow.

Why it is worth paying for

They are overpaying and cannot see where. Nothing else reads their own machine and proves which work is safe to move. And Nebius serves from Finland, so the same product answers the European question: this code never leaves the EU.

Reselling inference at a margin would pay us more when you spend more. We would rather be paid when the bill goes down.
